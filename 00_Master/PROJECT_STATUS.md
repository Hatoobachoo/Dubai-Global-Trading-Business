## Current Evidence-Based Position

1. No broker, API, licence route or final business model is approved yet.
2. A branded portal connected to licensed broker infrastructure remains a lead hypothesis only.
3. UAE mainland official material separates Introduction from OTC-derivatives / Spot-FX brokerage.
4. Current mainland First Category rules show at least AED 30m paid-up capital for the category that includes OTC/Spot-FX broker activity.
5. DIFC/DFSA permissions are granular (arranging, dealing, retail clients, client assets); exact OTC CFD/FX perimeter remains to be formally mapped.
6. Technology/API capability is not treated as proof of regulatory permission.

## Immediate Research Gate

- exact current Category 5 mainland requirements and costs;
- exact DFSA treatment of proposed OTC gold/FX CFD instruments and workflow;
- which brokers explicitly permit third-party branded client-facing trading;
- initial country-access strategy and total Year-1 capital requirement.
